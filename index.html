What is Below?

BELOW is a spine-chilling VR horror game built for Meta Quest. You wake up in a decaying forest overrun with twisted trees and forgotten ruins. The air is thick. The ground trembles.


Explore the Danger Forest — a quarantined zone where the boundary between our world and something far more sinister has shattered. Navigate treacherous paths, uncover dark secrets, and survive what lies beneath.


Every shadow hides a threat. Every sound could be your last warning. Do not look behind you.
