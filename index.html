About the Game

Step into a world beyond

Below is an immersive VR adventure game built for Meta Quest. Wake up in a sprawling forest full of towering trees, mysterious structures, and paths that lead somewhere unexpected.

Explore the Danger Forest zone, navigate winding trails through dense woodlands, and discover what lies beyond the fences. Every corner holds something new.

System & VR Requirements

Detailed hardware specifications, boundary modes, and Virtual Reality Check (VRC) compliance standards for Below.

Hardware & Target Devices Verified
  • Supported Headsets Meta Quest 2, Quest 3, Quest 3S, Pro
  • Controllers Touch / Touch Plus (6DoF)
  • Target Refresh Rate 72 Hz / 90 Hz native
  • Storage Space ~1.2 GB required
  • Internet Connection Offline playable (Single-player)
Play Modes & Comfort App Lab
  • Play Area Standing, Roomscale, Seated
  • Boundary System Guardian / Room Boundary 6DoF
  • Locomotion Smooth movement & Teleport
  • Turning Options Snap turn (30°/45°) & Smooth turn
  • Comfort Rating Comfortable / Moderate
