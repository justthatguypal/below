Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026 • Developer: BelowTeam • Publisher: Foet555

1. Overview

BelowTeam ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you play Below on Meta Quest devices and related services.

This policy is designed to satisfy Meta Quest Store & App Lab Virtual Reality Check (VRC.Quest.Privacy.1) compliance guidelines.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection to ensure player privacy and smooth VR performance:

  • Gameplay Progress & Local Preferences: Settings such as locomotion style (teleport/smooth), audio volumes, comfort vignettes, and save states are stored locally on your Meta Quest headset.
  • Headset & System Diagnostics: Hardware type (e.g. Meta Quest 2, Meta Quest 3, Quest Pro), framerate performance, and crash logs may be collected through standard Meta platform analytics to help us diagnose bugs and maintain framerate targets (72/90/120 Hz).
  • User Identity: Your Meta User ID or username may be utilized for local player profiles and leaderboard/multiplayer integration if enabled.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect raw microphone audio without your consent.
  • We do NOT collect or store Guardian/Boundary passthrough camera data or room scans.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to third-party advertisers.

4. Meta Platform Data

As a title operating within the Meta Quest ecosystem, certain data processing is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy. For more information regarding Meta account data handling, visit Meta's Privacy Center.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All local game saves and preference files can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the application from your Meta Quest library or clearing the application data from Quest headset storage settings.
