Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026 • Developer: BelowTeam • Publisher: Foet555 • Application: Below (Virtual Reality)

✓ Meta Virtual Reality Checks (VRC) Compliance

This Privacy Policy specifically addresses Virtual Reality Check criteria VRC.Privacy.1, VRC.Privacy.2, VRC.Privacy.3, and VRC.Privacy.4 as required for Meta Horizon Store and Meta Quest App Lab publication.

1. Identification & Public Access VRC.Privacy.1

This Privacy Policy is publicly hosted and maintained by BelowTeam (Publisher: Foet555) for the application Below on Meta Quest. This policy applies to all users who download, install, access, or interact with Below via Meta Quest, Meta Horizon Store, App Lab, or connected online services.

Our official support channel is accessible via our Discord community at discord.gg/8hZ2F2qTVC and our project repository at github.com/justthatguypal/below.

2. User Data Collection & Scope of Processing VRC.Privacy.2

We believe in data minimization. Below collects only the data strictly necessary for game functionality, stability, and player safety:

  • Local Game Data & Preferences: Your locomotion choices (smooth vs. teleport), audio volume levels, graphics settings, comfort vignettes, and game progression are stored locally on your headset device storage.
  • Hardware & Performance Diagnostics: Device model (Meta Quest 2, Quest 3, Quest 3S, Quest Pro), runtime framerate, memory usage, and crash dumps collected via Meta's standard platform diagnostic APIs to ensure adherence to 72/90/120 Hz performance standards.
  • Meta Account & Identity: If logged into a Meta Quest account, the application utilizes your Oculus/Meta User ID for player identification, save file association, and leaderboard rankings where applicable.
  • Sensor & Camera Data Disclosure: Below does NOT access, record, process, or store raw headset camera feeds, passthrough footage, Guardian room mapping data, or biometric eye/face tracking raw metrics.
  • Microphone Audio: Microphone audio is never recorded, stored, or analyzed on developer servers.

3. Data Retention, User Rights & Deletion Mechanism VRC.Privacy.3

We respect your right to control, inspect, and delete your personal and gameplay information:

  • Local Data Deletion: You can completely erase all locally stored data, preferences, and save files at any time by uninstalling Below from your Meta Quest library or navigating to Settings → Storage → Below → Delete App Data on your headset.
  • Cloud & Server Data Deletion Requests: If you wish to request the deletion of any associated account records, bug reports, or leaderboard entries tied to your Meta User ID, you can submit a deletion request through:
    • The official website reporting tool at below/index.html#report
    • Our official Discord support server: https://discord.gg/8hZ2F2qTVC
    All verified deletion requests are processed within 30 days without charge.

4. Children's Privacy & Age Assurance VRC.Privacy.4

Below is designed and distributed in compliance with Meta Quest platform age policies and applicable child protection laws (including the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act - COPPA and GDPR-K):

  • Below does not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal data from children under the minimum age specified by Meta account requirements (typically 13 years of age, or 10-12 where parent-managed Meta accounts are supported).
  • If we discover that a user under the legal age has provided personal data without verified parental consent through the Meta ecosystem, we will promptly delete that data from our records.
  • Parents or legal guardians may contact us directly via Discord (discord.gg/8hZ2F2qTVC) to review, request deletion of, or refuse further collection of their child's information.

5. Security & Policy Changes

We implement standard security measures to protect any transmitted reports or diagnostics. If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revision date will be reflected at the top of this page, and material updates will be disclosed through app release notes on Meta Quest App Lab.
